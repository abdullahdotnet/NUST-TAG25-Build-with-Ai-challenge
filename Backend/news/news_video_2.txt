Script: In another development... Jalen Brunson, the New York Knicks' point guard, once again proved his clutch capabilities, leading his team to victory over the Detroit Pistons in Game 6 of their first-round playoff series.  While Brunson experienced a relatively subdued fourth quarter initially, he ultimately delivered when it mattered most, breaking Detroit's hearts and securing the Knicks' advancement.  His performance solidified his claim as a frontrunner for the Clutch Player of the Year award.  Despite a challenging final period, Brunson's timely scoring and playmaking proved decisive in the tense final minutes. The Knicks now move on to the next round, thanks in large part to their star point guard's heroics.  CBS Sports reports on Brunson's game-winning performance.
